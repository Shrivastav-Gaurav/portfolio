Privacy Policy

AllThingsBiznTech · Last updated: 22 November 2026

About

AllThingsBiznTech is a personal publishing tool. Its owner, Gaurav Shrivastav, uses it to publish content he has reviewed and approved to his own LinkedIn profile. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data accessed

  • The owner's LinkedIn member identifier and basic profile information, used to identify the account that posts.
  • An access token issued by LinkedIn, used only to publish approved posts.
  • The text and images of drafts and posts created by the owner.

The tool does not read other members' data, send messages, or interact with other members' content.

How data is used

Data is used solely to publish content to the owner's own LinkedIn profile. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

Storage and security

Access tokens are kept in a protected secret store and are deleted when access is revoked. Drafts are retained only as long as needed to schedule and publish them.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from LinkedIn under Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services. To request deletion of any stored data, email the address below.
